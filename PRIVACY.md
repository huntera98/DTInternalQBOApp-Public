# Privacy Policy

**Atkins CPA LLC -- Migration Tool**

*Last updated: March 16, 2026*

## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Atkins CPA LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Atkins CPA - Migration Tool ("Application"). This Application is a private internal tool and is not publicly distributed.

## 2. Information We Access

When you authorize this Application to connect to a QuickBooks Online company, the Application may access:

- **Account data:** Chart of accounts, account names, numbers, types, and balances
- **Department/Location data:** Department names and identifiers
- **Transaction data:** Journal entries, general ledger details, and related financial records
- **Company information:** Company name and realm ID for connection verification

## 3. How We Use Information

Information accessed through the QuickBooks Online API is used exclusively to:

- Map source accounting data to the correct QuickBooks Online accounts and departments
- Create journal entries and transactions in the connected QuickBooks Online company
- Validate that imported data matches source records
- Troubleshoot errors during the migration process

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- **Local storage only.** All data is processed and stored on local machines controlled by Atkins CPA LLC. No QuickBooks Online data is uploaded to cloud servers, third-party platforms, or external databases.
- **OAuth tokens** are stored in local JSON files with restricted file system permissions. Tokens are never shared, transmitted, or stored in publicly accessible locations.
- **Temporary cache files** (account ID mappings, migration logs) are created during migration runs and retained only as long as needed for the engagement.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share QuickBooks Online data accessed through this Application with any third party. Data is accessible only to authorized personnel of Atkins CPA LLC performing the authorized migration work.

## 6. Data Retention

- OAuth access tokens expire after 1 hour and are refreshed as needed.
- OAuth refresh tokens are valid for up to 100 days.
- Local cache and mapping files are retained for the duration of the client engagement and deleted upon completion.
- No long-term storage of QuickBooks Online data occurs outside of what resides in the client's own QuickBooks Online company file.

## 7. Your Rights

As the QuickBooks Online company administrator, you may:

- **Revoke access** at any time by disconnecting this Application from your QuickBooks Online company via Settings > Manage Apps in QuickBooks Online
- **Request deletion** of any locally stored data by contacting us directly
- **Review** what data has been accessed or imported by examining the QuickBooks Online audit log

## 8. Intuit Platform Compliance

This Application complies with Intuit's developer platform requirements, including:

- Use of OAuth 2.0 for authentication and authorization
- Adherence to Intuit's Terms of Service for Developer Services
- Proper handling and protection of customer data as outlined in Intuit's security requirements

## 9. Children's Privacy

This Application is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
